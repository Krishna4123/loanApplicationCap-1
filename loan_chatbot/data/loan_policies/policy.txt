# Loan Policy for Educational Purpose

## 1. Introduction
This document outlines the policies for personal and educational loans.

## 2. Personal Loans
- **Interest Rate**: 12% per annum.
- **Max Amount**: $50,000.
- **Tenure**: 1 to 5 years.
- **Eligibility**:
  - Minimum Age: 21 years.
  - Minimum Income: $30,000 per year.
  - Credit Score: > 650.

## 3. Educational Loans
- **Interest Rate**: 6% per annum (subsidized).
- **Max Amount**: $100,000.
- **Tenure**: Up to 15 years.
- **Eligibility**:
  - Must be enrolled in an accredited university.
  - Co-signer required if income < $20,000.

## 4. Documentation
- Proof of Identity (Passport/ID).
- Proof of Income (Payslips/Tax Returns).
- Admission Letter (for Education Loan).

## 5. Approval Process
1. Submit Application.
2. Verification (2-3 business days).
3. Approval & Disbursement (1-2 business days).

## 6. Foreclosure
- No penalty for foreclosure after 6 months of EMI payment.
- 2% penalty if foreclosed within 6 months.
