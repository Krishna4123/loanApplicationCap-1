
LOANS – Section 3.2 (Condensed Version)
Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation

INTRODUCTION
Establishes safety and soundness standards for insured depository institutions. Emphasizes the importance of loan portfolio quality, lending practices, and examiner evaluation in managing credit risk.

LOAN ADMINISTRATION

Lending Policies
Boards of directors are responsible for establishing and approving written lending policies. Policies should define lending authority, loan types, collateral standards, appraisal guidelines, credit file maintenance, collection procedures, portfolio limits, risk diversification, loan review systems, and allowance for credit losses.

Loan Review Systems
Loan review systems identify credit weaknesses, support allowance estimation, monitor trends, evaluate lending personnel, ensure policy and regulatory compliance, and provide objective portfolio assessments.

Credit Risk Rating or Grading Systems
Credit grading identifies problem loans and assigns risk ratings. Independent review of grades is encouraged. Systems should include formal grading, identification of problem loans, reporting mechanisms, and documentation of credit loss experience.

Loan Review System Elements
Defines review policies including personnel qualifications, independence, review frequency, scope, depth, follow-up, and reporting.

Qualifications of Loan Review Personnel
Personnel should be experienced, trained, and knowledgeable in lending practices and regulations.

Loan Review Personnel Independence
Reviews should be conducted independently from loan approval to avoid conflicts of interest.

Frequency of Reviews
Significant credits are reviewed at least annually or more frequently if risk increases.

Scope of Reviews
Includes large loans and smaller loans with elevated risk characteristics.

Depth of Reviews
Reviews assess credit quality, documentation, collateral, approvals, covenant compliance, and accuracy of risk ratings.

Review of Findings and Follow-up
Findings are discussed with management and unresolved issues reported to the board.

Workpaper and Report Distribution
Review results are documented and reported regularly to the board.

Current Expected Credit Losses (CECL)
CECL requires estimation of expected credit losses over the contractual life of financial assets using past events, current conditions, and forecasts.

Allowance for Loan and Lease Losses (ALLL)
Institutions must maintain an allowance sufficient to absorb estimated credit losses. ALLL covers individual and collective loan risks.

Responsibility of the Board and Management
Management and boards must maintain appropriate allowance levels, ensure timely charge-offs, and support allowance estimates with documentation.

Factors to Consider in Estimating Credit Losses
Includes economic conditions, lending policies, portfolio composition, management quality, loan performance, and credit concentrations.

Examiner Responsibilities
Examiners assess the appropriateness of the allowance and the effectiveness of related systems.

Regulatory Reporting of the ALLL
ALLL must be reported accurately in regulatory reports and adjusted when inadequate.

Accounting and Reporting Treatment
Provides accounting guidance for recognizing and measuring loan impairment under U.S. GAAP.
